Synopsis & Context

Commissioned by prestigious design studio Lumina, this architectural profile is structured around the path of natural sunlight across 24 hours. The camera mimics architectural perspective lines, utilizing custom-built mechanical sliders for imperceptibly slow, hypnotic motion.

The Creative Challenge

Reflecting precise material textures—raw off-form concrete, oxidised copper, and honed travertine—without clipping delicate highlights.

Cinematic Direction

Meditative and structured. The rhythm matches the calm ocean swells just meters away.

Technical Production Notes:
  • •Shot exclusively with natural lighting and minimal negative fill to honour real architectural lived experience.
