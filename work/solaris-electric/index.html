Synopsis & Context

A high-stakes commercial campaign broadcast during national sporting finals and across digital channels. The film contrasts the silent stillness of electric propulsion with the roar of crashing surf, framed by dramatic golden hour aerials along the Great Ocean Road.

The Creative Challenge

Managing rolling road closures across 45 kilometres of iconic heritage coastline under tight police-escort schedules.

Cinematic Direction

Futuristic yet organic. The deep teal curves of the vehicle absorb coastal sea spray, creating a tactile commercial look.

Technical Production Notes:
  • •Helicopter gimbal tracking rig deployed for wide coastal cliff sweeps.
  • •Custom sound design blending wind harmonics with synthetic motor frequencies.
