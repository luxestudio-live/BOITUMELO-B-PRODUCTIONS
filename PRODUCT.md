# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 14 (App Router), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Motion for React, Lucide Icons

## Users

1. **Commercial Brands & Agencies:** Marketing directors, brand managers, and creative agency producers seeking high-craft visual cinema, product aesthetics, and national commercial campaigns.
2. **Artists & Musicians:** Recording artists, independent bands, and record labels seeking expressive, boundary-pushing music videos, live performance films, and visual worldbuilding.
3. **Documentary & Cultural Collaborators:** Cultural heritage trusts, arts councils, and non-fiction filmmakers seeking authentic narrative direction, patient storytelling, and respectful collaboration on Country across Australia.

## Product Purpose

BOITUMELO B PRODUCTIONS exists to provide an elite, cinematic digital flagship experience that establishes the studio's creative identity, showcases its body of work with immense visual fidelity, and converts prospective collaborators into committed productions through a structured 5-step creative brief workflow. Success means establishing instant creative authority, demonstrating out-of-distribution craft, and generating qualified project enquiries without relying on generic videographer tropes.

## Positioning

An Australian creative production house operating at the intersection of independent documentary depth and commercial visual cinema. Unlike commoditized video agencies or solo camera operators, the studio positions itself as a dedicated director and narrative collaborator specializing in *"Stories Worth Feeling"*—films that move people emotionally and move brands commercially.

## Operating Context

- **Geographic Footprint:** Headquartered in Australia with active productions spanning Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane, regional communities, and remote Outback terrain (Flinders Ranges, Central Desert).
- **Field Workflows:** Self-sufficient off-grid solar power packs, 4WD remote logistics, CASA-certified drone operations, and cultural consent protocols with First Nations communities.
- **Exhibition Standards:** DCI-compliant DCP festival packages, ProRes 4444 archival masters, and 5.1 / ambisonic audio mastering.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Confirmed Capabilities:**
  - 4 core production disciplines (Documentary & Film, Music Videos, Corporate Video, Brand & Commercial Cinema).
  - Full-screen edge-to-edge video showreel with user audio toggle.
  - Interactive portfolio archive with client-side category filtering and hover video previews.
  - Dynamic case-study detail routes (`/work/[slug]`) with custom cinema player, metadata matrix, and behind-the-frames archival stills.
  - 5-step collaborative creative brief builder (`/book`) with provisional scheduling picker.
  - Direct studio enquiry interface (`/contact`) with Australian service footprint details.
  - Production CMS preview (`/admin`) for managing projects, services, testimonials, media, and inbound leads.
- **Technical Constraints:**
  - Frontend-only prototype scope for current milestone (local state and simulated submissions; live cloud database and authentication deferred to future backend implementation).
- **Undecided Product Facts:**
  - Physical studio street address (intentionally omitted; framed Australia-wide).
  - Verified commercial pricing tiers (currently structured as budget ranges).
  - Finalized client video asset hosting CDN.

## Product Principles

1. **Finish the Job with Uncompromising Craft:** Every digital surface must demonstrate the same production discipline, lighting reverence, and attention to detail that the studio delivers in its films.
2. **Narrative Truth Over Cliché:** Reject exaggerated marketing jargon, generic videographer templates, and decorative visual gimmicks that lack purpose.
3. **Collaborative Partnership:** Treat prospective clients not as administrative tickets, but as creative co-conspirators guided through structured storytelling briefs.
4. **Cultural Reverence & Commercial Rigor:** Balance respectful cultural protocols and deep human listening with punctuality, clear deliverables, and commercial accountability.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Adherence to WCAG 2.2 AA standards with contrast ratios exceeding requirements (`21:1` white-on-black, `7.2:1` gold-on-black).
- Visible keyboard focus indicators (`*:focus-visible`).
- Global `prefers-reduced-motion` compliance to respect visitors with vestibular sensitivities.
- Accessible HTML5 video players with visible audio toggles and no unexpected autoplay sound.
